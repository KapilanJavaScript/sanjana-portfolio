About

Sanjana is an Indian fashion and editorial model. Her gaze moves between fragility and structure — tenderly feminine, quietly strong. Natural skin, dark hair, a braid that reads as discipline; clothes that frame rather than shout.

A study in warmth: editorial beauty, movement, textile and line. Available for campaigns, test shoots, showrooms and collaborations worldwide.
